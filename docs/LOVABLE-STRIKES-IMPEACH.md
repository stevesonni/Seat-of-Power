# Lovable art: strikes, flights and the President's impeachment

**Delivered.** The two jets came in their own pack (Seat_of_Power_Flight_Jets.zip) and fly now; pack 25 brought the other 17, all in use (pack 25's own jets were not used, as the owner asked).

The game already works with stand-in pictures. When these arrive, Claude puts each one where it belongs.

**Style:** as the rest of the pack: flat-shaded cartoon with bold outlines. All people are fictional. No real coat of arms, seals, flags or party logos. No text on the pictures. WebP.

## 1. Aircraft for the flight animation (public/art/objects/)
These fly across the sky exactly like `airliner-side.webp` does now. Each must match it: a side view, nose to the right, transparent background, the same size and framing (about 1600 × 600), and the same angle and lighting, so the plane can be swapped without the animation changing.

| File | The picture | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| military-jet-side.webp | An Air Force transport jet in grey military livery, with invented roundels | The President flies from the Air Force base during a strike |
| private-jet-side.webp | A small white business jet with an invented stripe and no logos | A governor charters a jet during a strike |

The jet sound is in: `public/audio/sfx/military-jet.mp3` (left to right, flying out) and `military-jet-home.mp3` (channels swapped, flying home).
