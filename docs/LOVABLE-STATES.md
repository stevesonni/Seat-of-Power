# Lovable art: each state's own programmes and bills

**Delivered (22 Nov 2026): all 40 are in `public/art/variants/` and the game uses them (`VARIANT_ART` reads `SYS.VARIANT_ART_ALL`).**

Programmes and bills now change with the state. Rural electrification in Abia means working with Aba's ring-fenced power company. In Lagos you license your own mini-grids. In Bayelsa the panels come by boat, and in Zamfara the sites need guards. Each version needs its own picture. Until a picture arrives, the game shows the programme's usual picture, so nothing breaks.

## Rules (as docs/LOVABLE-DECISIONS.md)
- Painted, warm, semi-realistic style, the same as the other decision cards. Not 3D, not photographic.
- 1600×900 (16:9), WebP (or PNG), no transparency. Keep the subject in the middle 60%: the card crops the edges on phones.
- Nigeria, as it really looks in that part of the country: creeks and stilt houses in the Niger Delta, red earth and gullies in the South-East, savannah and mud-brick in the far north, Lagos lagoon towns.
- No real people. No readable words, logos, party symbols, crests, flags with crests, or company branding. Security uniforms must be invented: plain colours, no badges or insignia.
- Nobody hurt, no weapons pointed at anyone, nothing gory.
- File names exactly as below. Put every file in `public/art/variants/`.

## Programmes

### Rural electrification (5)
1. `rural_elec-ring.webp` (Abia). *A gas-fired power station at the edge of Aba, a busy Nigerian manufacturing town; in front, technicians in blue overalls fix new prepaid electricity meters to the walls of shops and houses while traders watch. Late afternoon, power lines lead into town.*
2. `rural_elec-gully.webp` (Anambra, Imo, Enugu, Ebonyi). *Linemen in hard hats raise new concrete electricity poles along a red-earth road in a hilly South-East Nigerian village; the poles curve around a deep, raw erosion gully at the roadside. Green hills, palm trees, villagers watching from the far side.*
3. `rural_elec-lagoon.webp` (Lagos). *A solar mini-grid on a raised platform beside a lagoon fishing community near Epe, Lagos: rows of panels, a small battery house, wooden canoes pulled up on the shore, children in front of zinc-roofed houses with their first lit bulbs at dusk.*
4. `rural_elec-sun.webp` (the far north, the savannah). *A large solar farm in dry, flat savannah under a hard blue sky; beyond it a village of mud-brick houses with thatched granaries; a herder walks cattle past the fence; engineers in reflective vests check the panels.*
5. `rural_elec-creek.webp` (Bayelsa, Rivers, Delta, Akwa Ibom). *A Niger Delta creek village of houses on stilts; solar panels on a raised wooden platform above the water; a long boat carrying more panels and cables pulls up to the jetty; mangroves, still brown water, a distant gas flare on the horizon.*

### Clean water (4)
6. `water-deep.webp` (the far north). *A solar-powered deep borehole in a dry northern Nigerian village: a tall steel tank on a stand, a row of taps, women and girls filling yellow jerrycans, cracked dry earth, a neem tree for shade.*
7. `water-creek.webp` (the Delta). *A small water treatment plant on stilts beside a creek town in the Niger Delta: white tanks and pipes, a worker testing a sample in a glass, children with buckets at a new tap, the brown creek behind.*
8. `water-city.webp` (Lagos). *Workers lay large blue water pipes in a dug trench along a busy Lagos street; danfo buses squeeze past; a water vendor with a cart of jerrycans watches, unhappy; city buildings behind.*
9. `water-hill.webp` (the South-East). *A pumping station and a raised water tank on a green hilltop above a South-East Nigerian town; a pipeline runs down the slope beside an erosion gully held back by stone gabions.*

### Roads (4)
10. `roads-swamp.webp` (the Delta). *Sand-filling a swamp to build a road in the Niger Delta: a dredger pumps wet sand through long pipes onto a new embankment through mangroves; bulldozers flatten it; workers in boots and helmets.*
11. `roads-gully.webp` (the South-East). *A new road in a South-East Nigerian town with deep concrete side drains and a large repaired gully beside it, stepped with stone gabions and young grass; engineers with a plan; red earth.*
12. `roads-savannah.webp` (the North). *A grader and a roller build a red laterite road across open northern savannah; a police pickup waits at the roadside as escort; a village of round mud houses in the distance; dust in golden light.*
13. `roads-city.webp` (Lagos). *Night road works on a Lagos expressway under floodlights: workers lay asphalt in a fenced-off lane while traffic crawls past on a diversion, with tail lights, hawkers and a pedestrian bridge.*

### Farms (5)
14. `irrigation-fadama.webp` (the dry north). *A river valley in northern Nigeria in the dry season: green onion and tomato beds watered by small solar pumps and hoses, farmers in caps and wrappers at work, a dry brown landscape all around.*
15. `irrigation-palm.webp` (Abia, Imo, Edo). *A modern palm oil mill among oil palm plantations in South-East Nigeria: steel presses and boilers under a roof, workers in boots, women bringing palm fruit bunches by wheelbarrow, red palm oil flowing into drums.*
16. `irrigation-cocoa.webp` (Ondo, Osun, Ekiti, Cross River). *A cocoa farmers' cooperative in a rainforest village in South-West Nigeria: cocoa beans drying on long raised racks under a new shed, a covered warehouse, farmers weighing sacks on a scale.*
17. `irrigation-ricemill.webp` (Ebonyi, Kebbi, Niger). *A new rice mill beside rice fields: paddy drying on a concrete floor, a steel mill building, women bagging clean white rice, farmers bringing sacks by tricycle.*
18. `farm-fish.webp` (Lagos and the Delta). *A fish farm by a lagoon: rows of round tanks and floating cages, workers netting catfish, a new cold room with a solar roof, wooden boats tied at the edge.*

### Schools and health (6)
19. `school_feed-north.webp`. *Pupils in a northern Nigerian public primary school sit on mats in the shade eating hot meals from steel bowls: tuwo, beans soup and a boiled egg; cooks in hijabs serve from big pots.*
20. `school_feed-south.webp`. *Pupils in a southern Nigerian public primary school eat at long benches: rice, stew, fish and a boiled egg on plastic plates; women cooks in aprons with big pots; a green school building behind.*
21. `free_edu-almajiri.webp` (the North). *A Qur'anic school in northern Nigeria brought into the school system: boys hold wooden Qur'anic slates and new exercise books, a mallam and a young teacher stand together at a blackboard with simple sums (no readable words), a new classroom wall behind.*
22. `classrooms-raised.webp` (flood and creek states). *A new classroom block raised on concrete pillars above flood level in a riverine Nigerian town, with stairs and a ramp; floodwater lingers under it; pupils in uniform climb the stairs; a canoe tied nearby.*
23. `classrooms-fenced.webp` (bandit areas). *A new classroom block in a rural northern Nigerian school behind a high perimeter wall and a gate; a uniformed guard (invented uniform, no badges) at the gate; pupils entering; savannah beyond.*
24. `phc-boat.webp` (the Delta). *A floating clinic: a white boat with a covered deck fitted as a clinic, moored at a creek village jetty; a nurse in a white uniform weighs a baby while mothers wait on the jetty.*

### Security corps (4)
All uniforms invented: plain colours, no badges, no insignia, no readable words.
25. `amotekun-west.webp` (the South-West). *Community security guards in khaki-and-green uniforms with reflective vests on patrol at the edge of a South-West Nigerian town at dusk, on foot and on motorcycles, with torches; townspeople greeting them.*
26. `amotekun-east.webp` (the South-East). *Community security guards in dark green uniforms at a checkpoint on a forest road in South-East Nigeria; a trader's bus stopped and waved through; palm forest on both sides.*
27. `amotekun-north.webp` (the North-West). *Community guards in brown uniforms on motorcycles ride in a line along a savannah track toward a village of mud houses; village elders wave them in.*
28. `amotekun-civilian.webp` (Borno, Yobe). *Local volunteers in mixed civilian clothes and plain vests escort women collecting firewood at the edge of a dry north-eastern town; a calm, watchful scene.*

## Bills (10)
29. `bill-ranching.webp`: an anti-open grazing law that is finally enforced. *A fenced cattle ranch in Nigeria with water troughs and a vet; rangers in plain uniforms; farmers' green fields untouched on the other side of the fence.*
30. `bill-grazing_reserve.webp`: the northern grazing reserves law. *A large grazing reserve in northern Nigeria: a new borehole and water trough, a vet vaccinating cattle, Fulani herders resting under a tree, marked cattle routes of white posts.*
31. `bill-family_court.webp`: the family courts law. *A small, calm family court room: a judge in a dark robe at a low desk, a child-protection officer kneeling beside a girl and her mother, no cage, no crowd.*
32. `bill-sarc.webp`: sexual assault referral centres. *A quiet, well-lit clinic room for survivors: a nurse and a counsellor talk with a woman whose back is to the viewer, soft chairs, a closed door. Respectful; nothing graphic.*
33. `bill-power_market.webp`: the state's own power market. *Officials of a state electricity regulator at a long table with meter samples and a map of power lines; through the window, a substation and power lines. Nothing readable.*
34. `bill-host_trust.webp`: host community trusts in oil states. *Elders, women and youths of a Niger Delta community at a meeting under a big tree by a creek, counting a trust fund's ledger with an official; a gas flare far behind.*
35. `bill-erosion.webp`: the erosion control law. *Surveyors with poles and a tripod measure a vast erosion gully in South-East Nigeria; houses stand dangerously near its edge; red earth; a new warning line of white posts.*
36. `bill-flood.webp`: the flood plain law. *A riverside Nigerian town after the rains: officials paint marks on houses within the flood line, a new raised relief camp of tents on high ground, a gauge post in the river.*
37. `bill-waterways.webp`: the waterways safety law. *A busy jetty in a creek or lagoon town: passengers in orange life jackets board a licensed passenger boat, an inspector checks the boat, morning light on the water.*
38. `bill-forest_guards.webp`: the forest guards law. *Local hunters recruited as forest guards, in plain brown uniforms with traditional hunting gear, gather at the edge of a dry northern forest with a village leader; dawn.*
39. `bill-hisbah.webp`: the Hisbah board law. *A board meeting in a northern Nigerian government office: clerics in turbans, officials in kaftans, a police officer and two women officers in hijabs around a table. Calm, formal.*
40. `bill-revenue.webp`: the revenue law. *A neat state revenue office: traders pay at a counter with a card machine and get printed receipts, with no touts outside; a market street through the window.*
