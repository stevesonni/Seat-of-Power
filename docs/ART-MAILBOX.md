# Mailbox and Chief of Staff: art brief

The Chief of Staff currently borrows commissioner portraits. These pictures would give them their own.

## Match the existing portraits
- **Style:** the same painted style and lighting as `characters/special-adviser.webp`.
- **Framing:** taller than wide, from the head to the knees, plain soft background.
- **People:** all fictional. No logos or coats of arms.
