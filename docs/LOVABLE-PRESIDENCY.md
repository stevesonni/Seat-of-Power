# For Lovable: Pack 14, the Villa

The Presidency is now playable: winning the race swears the player in at Eagle Square and they govern Nigeria from Aso Rock. It already uses the presidency pack (Villa office, Federal Executive Council, situation room, state banquet, the Central Bank governor, the army chief, the Governors' Forum chair, the fuel queue, the inauguration and the national broadcast). This pack makes the President's world as rich as the governor's. Please send it as one zip, with every file named exactly as listed.

**Rules:** the same as every pack: the game's painted style; all people fictional (no real presidents, ministers, generals or lookalikes); no real coats of arms, logos or foreign flags (green-white-green is fine); buildings may suggest real places but plainly, with no crests or signage. Backgrounds and scenes 1280×720 with no text; characters on a transparent background, about 700 px tall, with moods as the same person in the same outfit.

## The Villa compound (`compound/`)
The governor's Desk is a painted map of the state capital's compound (`compound-full.webp`, 1500×850). The President needs their own.
- `compound-villa`: 1500×850, a landscape map of the presidential villa and the Three Arms Zone, seen from above at an angle: the Villa office block, the Federal Executive Council chamber, the banquet hall, a helipad, the National Assembly's dome in the distance, the Supreme Court, a press briefing room, gardens and a guarded gate. Leave clear open ground in front of each building (the game places buttons there).

## Scenes (`scenes/`)
- `presidential-jet`: a green-and-white presidential airliner on the apron at night, steps down, a guard of honour and a red carpet.
- `guard-of-honour`: a visiting head of state inspecting a guard of honour at the Villa forecourt.
- `fec-meeting`: ministers around the Federal Executive Council table, the President's chair at the head (seen from behind).
- `un-podium`: the President speaking at a large international assembly podium (no UN logo, no real hall).
- `coup-night`: armoured vehicles on a dark city avenue near government buildings, soldiers, one radio mast.
- `governors-forum`: thirty-six governors seated in a horseshoe around a long table.
- `nass-joint-session`: the President presenting the budget to a joint session of the National Assembly.
- `national-grid-dark`: a city at night half blacked out, a transmission tower in front.
- `idp-return`: families with bundles boarding buses home from a camp, soldiers escorting.

## Characters (`characters/`), each with `-pleased` and `-angry`
- `vice-president` (male, agbada) and `vice-president-female` (female, kaftan).
- `chief-justice`: in black robes and the full-bottomed wig.
- `inspector-general`: a senior police officer in dress uniform.
- `ecowas-envoy`: a West African diplomat in a suit.
- `foreign-head-of-state`: a visiting president (fictional country), in a suit with a sash.

## Objects (`objects/`)
- `presidential-seal`: a round gold seal with an eagle and green-white-green (not the real coat of arms).
- `budget-briefcase`: a green leather budget briefcase.
- `red-phone`: an old secure telephone on a desk.

## Badges (`badges/`)
- `badge-commander`: a gold eagle over crossed swords (survive a coup attempt).
- `badge-unifier`: six linked rings in green and gold (win all six zones).
- `badge-crisis-ended`: a broken chain in gold (end the national crisis).

# Pack 16: the Bakunda war

The presidency now has a war storyline across the term: unrest in the Republic of **Bakunda** (Nigeria's fictional northern neighbour), **General Voko**'s coup, his "Axis" pact with **Ostrava**, Nigeria's Army, Air Force or Navy sent against it, summits in Abuja and Geneva, a visit to **Washington**, war weariness, and a peace accord the National Assembly must enact. Everything below is fictional: no real leaders, no real flags, crests or seals (Bakunda's colours: maroon and sand; Ostrava's: slate grey and white).

## Characters (`characters/`), standing, transparent background, about 700 px tall
- `chief-of-army-staff-full-neutral`, `-full-pleased`, `-full-worried`, `-full-urgent`: the **same** Chief of Army Staff as `chief-of-army-staff`, full length, in four moods. He presents every Bakunda moment (like the adviser's full-length poses).
- `general-voko`, `general-voko-angry`, `general-voko-pleased`: Bakunda's junta leader, in a maroon-and-sand uniform with dark glasses.
- `ostrava-leader`, `ostrava-leader-angry`: Ostrava's head of state, slate-grey suit.
- `presidential-pilot`, `presidential-pilot-pleased`: the President's pilot, a Nigerian Air Force Group Captain in a flight jacket with wings, **saluting**, full length. He welcomes the President at the Air Fleet before every trip ("Good day, Your Excellency. The jet is fuelled…").
- `us-president`, `us-president-pleased`: a **fictional** President of the United States who resembles no real president, in a dark suit.

## Scenes (`scenes/`), 1280×720, no text
- `air-fleet-hangar`: inside the Presidential Air Fleet's hangar: the green-and-white jet with its steps down, a red carpet to the steps, ground crew, warm floodlights. The backdrop for the pilot's welcome.
- `oval-office`: **the President of Nigeria and the fictional US President seated in an oval office** (the curved walls, two sofas, a heavy desk by the windows), shaking hands for photographers. No real presidential seal: a plain carpet. This goes on the newspaper front page.
- `bakunda-coup`: tanks and soldiers in a capital's central square at dawn, a radio mast, a burning car.
- `refugees-border`: families with bundles crossing a dusty border post into Nigeria, Nigerian officials receiving them.
- `airlift-evacuation`: Nigerians boarding a green-and-white jet on a dark apron, soldiers guiding them.
- `axis-summit`: Voko and the Ostravan leader at a long table with two flags (maroon-and-sand, grey-and-white).
- `army-convoy`: Nigerian mechanised infantry (armoured vehicles) on a border road.
- `air-strike`: two jets over rugged highland camps.
- `navy-patrol`: Nigerian gunboats on a wide lake at dusk, a supply boat stopped.
- `ecowas-summit`: West African heads of state around a round table in Abuja.
- `geneva-conference`: a long conference table in a European hall, delegations with headphones.
- `war-weariness`: a barracks town at dusk: a funeral, a mother holding a soldier's photograph.
- `peace-signing`: the accord signed at a long table, Voko's envoy and the Nigerian President, flags behind.
- `treaty-vote`: the National Assembly voting, hands raised.

## Objects (`objects/`), transparent, about 1000 px
- `defence-file`: a thick buff folder stamped "SECRET" with a map clipped to it.

- `intelligence-cable`: a printed telegram on a desk.

## Badge (`badges/`)
