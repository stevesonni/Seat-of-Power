# The Seat of Power — Vatar listing

**Category:** Simulation (or Strategy)

**Short description** (120 characters max):
Win a governorship in Nigeria, then govern: budgets, godfathers, tribunals and every promise you made.

**Long description:**
You are a Nigerian politician with a war chest, a party ticket and a dream. Run a four-week campaign against a rival who fights back. Win, and the real work begins in Government House.

Pass budgets through a House of Assembly with its own grudges. Pay salaries or face strikes. Pick commissioners, and fire the crooked ones. Create new ministries when the state needs them, from Sports to Power. Finance roads, hospitals and universities through banks, bonds, the AfDB or a risky contractor deal. Answer the communities shouting for help before it costs you the zone. Survive EFCC files, election petitions and impeachment threats.

Every decision shows its result, and every half-year ends in a report on what changed. Your Wikipedia-style biography records it all. Earn honours, reach a second term, and see where your career ranks among other governors.

The parties, people and events are fictional.
