# For Lovable: art for the new careers

> **Delivered (all five packs).** The ladder and Senator art is in `public/art`. The LGA Chairman, Ambassador and Presidency art (plus the chairman, ambassador and statesman badges) waits in `art-staging/` until those careers are built, because the game ships only pictures it uses.

The game is growing from one office into a career ladder: **LGA Chairman → Governor → Senator → President**, with **Ambassador** as a side road. Please send each pack below as its own zip, with every file named exactly as listed. Any format is fine; I convert.

**Rules for every picture**
- **Style:** the same painted style as the game's existing art (the governor, speaker and commissioner portraits, the chamber and campaign scenes).
- **People:** all fictional. No real politicians or lookalikes, no real presidents, senate presidents or ministers.
- **Symbols:** no real logos, no party logos, no national coat of arms, no real flags of other countries. Green-white-green colours are fine. Buildings may suggest real places, but plainly, with no crests or signage.
- **Characters:** standing, from the knees up or full length, on a **transparent background**, about **700 px tall**, facing slightly toward the viewer. Where moods are listed, keep the same person and outfit and change only the expression and gesture.
- **Backgrounds and scenes:** **1280×720**, no text in the picture.
- **Objects:** transparent background, about **1000 px** on the longest side.
- **Icons:** the same set as the 48 icons: flat, chunky, bold dark outline, **512×512**, transparent.
- **Badges:** the same round-medal style as the 11 career badges, **512×512**, transparent.
