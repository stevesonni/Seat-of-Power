# Lovable art: the last month in office

When a governor or President finishes their time, the game now plays their last month before showing the record:

1. the commissioning rush;
2. the last Executive Council;
3. the midnight files;
4. the farewell broadcast;
5. 29 May (the oath, then the trip home).

Each beat needs its own picture. Until a picture arrives, the game shows a stand-in from the existing art, so nothing breaks. When you drop a file in, the game picks it up by itself.

## Rules (as docs/LOVABLE-DECISIONS.md)
- **Style:** painted, warm and semi-realistic, the same as the other decision cards. Not 3D, not photographic.
- **Format:** 1600×900 (16:9), WebP (or PNG), no transparency.
- **Framing:** keep the subject in the middle 60%, because the card crops the edges on phones.
- **Setting:** Nigeria as it really looks. Government House compounds, Abuja, a home town.
- **What must not appear:**
  - real people or real politicians;
  - readable words, logos or party symbols;
  - coats of arms, seals, or flags with a crest. A plain green-white-green flag is fine.
  - Uniforms must be invented: plain colours, no badges or insignia.
- **Mood:** an ending. Warm, dignified, a little sad. Nobody hurt, nothing gory.
- **File names and folder:** use the names exactly as below, and put every file in `public/art/farewell/`.

## The pictures (10)

**The governor's last month**
1. `commissioning-rush.webp`: the commissioning rush. *A Nigerian governor in agbada cuts a green-and-white ribbon in front of a new road and bridge. Aides hold umbrellas, a brass band plays, and photographers crowd around. Behind them, a second site is plainly still half-built: scaffolding and a crane, with workers watching. Late afternoon.*
2. `last-exco.webp`: the last Executive Council. *A long table in a Government House council chamber, the final meeting. Commissioners in suits and traditional attire stand for a group photograph around the seated governor. Files are closed and cups set down, some faces are moved, one man looks away. Warm evening light through tall windows.*
3. `midnight-files.webp`: the midnight files. *A governor's desk late at night under a single lamp. Piles of folders, a fountain pen, and an aide leaning in with yet another file to sign. Through the window, a dark compound and one guard. Quiet and tempting.*
4. `farewell-broadcast.webp`: the farewell broadcast. *A governor in formal attire speaks to a television camera in a wood-panelled office. A plain green-white-green flag stands behind him and studio lights are set up. The crew watches. A solemn, final moment.*
5. `handover-oath.webp`: 29 May at Government House. *An inauguration in a stadium: the new governor takes the oath with a hand on a book before a judge in robes. The outgoing governor stands to one side, applauding politely. The stands are full, with plain flags and a guard of honour in invented uniforms.*
6. `convoy-home.webp`: leaving Government House. *A convoy of black SUVs leaving through Government House gates for the last time. Staff line the drive and wave. One window is down, showing a hand raised in goodbye. Golden hour.*

**The President's last month**

7. `last-fec.webp`: the last Federal Executive Council. *The Federal Executive Council chamber in Abuja, the final meeting. Ministers stand for a group photograph around the seated President at a long horseshoe table. Moved faces, closed folders.*
8. `eagle-square-handover.webp`: 29 May at Eagle Square. *Eagle Square in Abuja: the new President takes the oath on a covered stage before the Chief Justice in robes. The outgoing President stands nearby. A huge crowd, plain flags, and a guard of honour in invented uniforms. Bright late-morning sun.*
9. `jet-home.webp`: the flight home. *A white presidential jet with no markings or words on the tarmac. The outgoing President waves from the top of the stairs, and former ministers wave from below. A long goodbye.*

**For both**

10. `hometown-welcome.webp`: home. *A crowd welcomes a returning former leader in his home town: drummers, dancers, women in matching aso-ebi, and an elderly teacher shaking his hand. Dust, palm trees, a modest family house with its gate open.*

## When they arrive
Drop the files in `public/art/farewell/`. Nothing else to change: `SYS.FAREWELL_ART` already names them, and the game uses each one as soon as it is there.
