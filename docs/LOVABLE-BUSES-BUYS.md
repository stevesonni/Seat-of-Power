# Lovable art: bus terminals, and things bought rather than built

**Delivered and installed (25 Nov 2026):** all four pictures, resized to 1280×720 like the other cards.

Four pictures. The game already checks for each file: until it arrives it shows a stand-in, and when you drop the file in, the game uses it by itself. Nothing else needs changing.

## Rules (as docs/LOVABLE-DECISIONS.md)
- **Style:** painted, warm and semi-realistic, the same as the other cards. Not 3D, not photographic.
- **Setting:** Nigeria as it really looks.
- **What must not appear:**
  - real people or real politicians;
  - readable words, logos, company names or party symbols (bus and van sides are plain colours, with no lettering);
  - coats of arms, seals, police or army insignia, or flags with a crest. A plain green-white-green flag is fine.
- **Mood:** everyday and hopeful. Nobody hurt.
- **Format:** 1600×900 (16:9), WebP (or PNG), no transparency. Keep the subject in the middle 60%, because phones crop the edges.

## The pictures

### 1. `public/art/decisions/bus_terminals.webp`: a state's city bus terminal (the governor's programme "Modern Bus Terminals")
*A new bus terminal in a Nigerian state capital at mid-morning: a long steel-and-glass canopy over numbered bays, city buses in plain yellow and green pulling in, commuters queueing on clean platforms, a row of small food and airtime kiosks, a tidy taxi rank and okadas waiting outside the gate. Bright, orderly, busy.*

### 2. `public/art/decisions/f_bus_terminals.webp`: the President's interstate terminal (the federal programme "Interstate bus terminals in every zone")
*A large interstate coach terminal on a federal highway: long white and silver luxury coaches (no names on them) at covered loading bays, travellers with suitcases and bags of foodstuff, porters with trolleys, a waiting hall with ceiling fans, families saying goodbye. Bigger and grander than picture 1, a gateway between regions. A plain green-white-green flag may fly over the entrance.*

### 3. `public/art/scenes/patrol-fleet.webp`: the patrol vehicles bought for the state
*A handover in a police yard: forty new patrol pickups and vans in plain dark blue with light bars on the roof, parked in neat rows, keys being handed to officers in plain uniforms (no badges, no lettering), a small canopy and chairs for the ceremony. Shiny and new.*

### 4. `public/art/scenes/cctv-install.webp`: security cameras being installed
*Technicians in plain orange vests on a cherry-picker fixing CCTV cameras and a small solar panel to a tall pole at a busy city junction in Nigeria; traffic, danfo buses and okadas below; a second pole already fitted in the background.*

## Where they appear
- 1 and 2: the programme's picture when you choose or review the bus terminal programme. These two programmes are the only ones without a picture.
- 3 and 4: the project card and its site picture while the vehicles are on order or the cameras are going up. Until these arrive, the game shows a police picture instead of a building site.
- The President's interstate terminals already have their film (`public/art/reels/interstatebus.mp4`, from Gemini). No icons are needed: programmes use the sector symbols.
